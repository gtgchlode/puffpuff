PuffPuff: Glow Puzzle — storm, home and friends

1. THE FADED GARDEN · 50–100 Glow wins · playable
A storm scattered the reef and put out its coral lights. PuffPuff follows the dark
trail, relights three gardens, and restores his cottage so it feels like home again.
Goal bar: mystery house outline + ?, then a check. Tap for the brief story/tasks.
Required route: 220 Lumes (about 55–74 wins at 3–4 Lumes each); straightforward 242.
Optional treasure chests and furnishings cost extra. No extra completion requirement.

2. THE HIDDEN REEF · 75–125 Glow wins · planned
PuffPuff lights hidden coral paths and rescues Bailey, a little clownfish lost in
the dark. Bailey is the new name for the draft previously called Bubba.

3. THE DEEP BLUE · planned
Awaken deep coral lights and guide a baby sea turtle back to its mom.
Provisional target 100–150 Glow wins; final pacing/layout not yet designed.

4. THE KELP MAZE · planned
Light a safe route through kelp and rescue Seabiscuit the seahorse.
Earlier 100–150-win target remains provisional until this later world is designed.

Targets are rounds needed per world, not exclusive ranges of puzzle numbers.
Only 100 Glow levels and the first Trail are currently built.
