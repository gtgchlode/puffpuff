puffpuff — slingshot currents + illustrated path trials
October 30, 2026

Open Pause → Settings → Playground. Choose Golf or Paths, then 1–8.
Golf defaults to the new slingshot/current collection. The New menu also
contains Original eight, preserving the earlier comparison.
The heart keeps your favorites. Each demo has Retry/Reset.
These are disposable native Swift trials: no real currency, life, quest or Bloom
changes. Only your favorite labels are remembered after closing the playground.

NEW GOLF: all eight use pull back from Puff Puff, then release.
Tap arrows before a shot; only Live currents lets the dial turn during flight.
Compare 0–3 Trail finds. Scroll anywhere outside Puff Puff; aiming starts on him.
1. Soft streams — gentle flows bend a physical shot. Finds clear bumpers and
   widen the streams.
2. Jet relay — land in a jet; it carries you through its center and sends you
   along its arrow. Finds clear bumpers and preset one jet each.
3. Two ways home — choose the left or right fork. The left route starts open;
   optional finds clear the shorter right approach.
4. One tide dial — one control rotates all currents together. Finds reveal
   route segments and widen the capture zones.
5. Pocket current — Move current, tap open water, then turn its arrow. Sling
   through the placed jet. Finds clear bumpers and enlarge its catch area.
6. Pulse tide — jets alternate on/off. Turn arrows and time your release.
   Finds lengthen the active window and clear bumpers.
7. Soft landing — two jets plus a slowing swirl near home. Finds expand the
   swirl and entrance and clear bumpers; the swirl does not aim the fish.
8. Live currents — turn the dial while swimming to redirect the next jet.
   Finds slow the ride, giving more thinking time, and clear bumpers.

The originals remain available in the golf collection menu:
GOLF: compare the same idea with 0, 1, 2 or 3 Trail finds.
1. Soft putt — drag toward the shot. Each find removes a bumper.
2. Slingshot — pull back, release. Finds remove bumpers and widen home.
3. Bubble stop — Go, then Stop at home. Finds slow movement and widen the timing window.
4. Aim + puff — lock a swinging aim, then lock power. Finds slow both meters and clear bumpers.
5. Bank builder — pick a numbered bank route, then Puff. One route begins open;
   each find opens another. A planning puzzle with automatic strength.
6. Current gates — rotate three currents into a route, then Puff. Each find
   sets one current for you. Almost all the challenge is preparing the route.
7. Draw a route — trace around coral to home. Finds remove coral and add ink.
8. Tap to send — tap a landing point; power is automatic. Finds remove blockers,
   so full exploration allows a simple tap on home.

Prototype payout in ALL eight golf trials:
1 shot = 12 Glow + hint + lighter; 2 = 8 Glow + hint; 3 = 5 Glow; 4 = 2 Glow.
No home within 4 shots = nothing. Retry freely. Successful rounds retain their
best score; Collect takes a single set, never the sum of repeated attempts.
Changing the Trail finds comparison starts a fresh comparison including its best.
These numbers are trial values, not changes to the real game's six-putt rule.

ILLUSTRATED PATHS: search for three snacks and three optional supply caches.
Each region has four things to uncover: food (2 Glow), a hidden supply cache
(4), light-restoring coral (1), and a blocked passage (3). Contents stay hidden
until you pay. All trial balances and discoveries are separate from real play.
Tap the artwork or its named action/price. Opened objects remain visible;
clearing lights the scene. The local Bloom refill adds 8 trial Glow.
1. Cove doors — a close-up room with several objects; clear the passage,
   then swim onward or return to an earlier cove.
2. Fog islands — a tall continuous reef; clearing the edge of darkness reveals
   neighboring places. Search side pockets before opening the next stretch.
3. Side swim — illustrated scenes arranged horizontally. Swipe between them,
   or use the Kelp/Shells/Coral controls after opening each passage.
4. Reef garden — a denser field of coral patches; uncover adjacent patches
   to expand your lit territory without moving room to room.
5. Depth dive — a vertical stack of increasingly deep shelves. Search each
   shelf and open its passage to reveal the one below; scroll back for finds.
6. Home + spokes — a coral cottage hub. Visit three illustrated regions in
   any order and uncover several local objects in each.
7. Sonar hunt — a field of dark hiding places. Spend 1 Glow to scan, then the
   displayed price to remove the revealed obstacle. No travel between rooms.
8. Current forks — search a close-up pocket, then ride a one-way current to
   the next. After the third pocket, loop back to search anything missed.

At three snacks, Go home unlocks Try these finds in golf. Extra local searches
remain possible before transferring. Each cache supplies one advantage in the
selected golf trial. Change the finds comparison to see how exploration helps.
The real Trail, Bloom progress, resources and six-putt bonus round are unchanged.


Enemy challenges (Settings → Playground)
1. Timed school — Two fish patrol. Time a single drag-and-release shot to sink both into the prison.
2. Scare enemies — Eight moves; caught fish stay in the hole.
3. Shell setup — Drag two shells before the first shot. Bank off at least one shell and sink both fish in three moves. Retry unlocks placement again.
All three use only local trial state. No real shots, Glow or progress are affected.

Settings → Replay kelp — The real introductory kelp geometry, free practice. Touch kelp to end the attempt; reach the hole within four shots. Retry as often as desired.

Golf aids (November 5): Kelp run and all three enemy challenge trials include
Current and Block controls with three free supplies each. Pick open water and
confirm placement. Retry restores the trial stock and clears placed aids;
production spends real collected supplies and does not refund them.
Timed-school release now uses the displayed school phase, and Retry swims
the fish back into their continuing patrol.
