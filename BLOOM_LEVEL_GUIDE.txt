APPROVED LEVELS 101–185 — November 9, 2026
Levels 1–100 and their saved attempts remain unchanged. Levels 101–149 allow 42 taps; 150–154 allow 34; 155–185 allow 42. No pearl targets are added in this extension.

Linked coral: tapping a member applies its normal Glow pulse and flips the other members of its same-color group once. Group members do not start a second ripple. Members share a hue, with a centered chain symbol on each linked coral and no connector line between them; separate groups use separate colors.
Caged coral cannot be tapped directly. Any ripple that reaches it can flip it, and it counts toward the all-lit win condition.
Coupled mini-board: the 3×3 or 5×5 main board keeps its normal Glow rule; the four colored coral also operate a separate 2×2 mini board. Only the shown next color flips its mini pattern; an out-of-order color still ripples on the main board. The mini-board starts with an alternating on/off pattern; the four ordered patterns leave every light on. Both boards must be all lit to win.
The first linked pair, cage, mini-board and expanded linked group each have a skippable, unnumbered practice before their introduction.

Linked members share a color and carry a centered same-color chain symbol; there are no connector lines. A linked tap flips every other group member once, without a second ripple. Caged coral cannot be tapped directly, but ripples can flip it and it must be lit to win.
