# puffpuff project guidance

Coral artwork must have no faces. Keep product copy sparse: no decorative subtitles, slogans, or repeated instructions. Keep short guidance when it teaches a new rule, explains a cost, or communicates an error. The user selected Lagoon: sunlit mint water, warm sand, organic sea-glass pebbles, and dark teal type. Refine this direction. Keep Honey Puffs low, wide and ruffled, with no tall central stalk. Chalkboard SE is the fixed app typeface, using regular and bold weights with Dynamic Type scaling. Settings has no font picker; old typeface preferences are ignored.

Before adding new gameplay mechanics, tile types, power-ups, or difficulty features, read `MECHANICS_CHECKLIST.md` in this directory. The user requested that these ideas be consulted when the app needs something new.

Treat unchecked items as a design backlog. Consult them during relevant feature work; implement them when that work calls for them. Protective covers are future board-supplied mechanics, not prizes; follow the latest cover rules below.

Keep the checklist current as decisions are made. Mark an idea implemented only when it is present in the app, and resolve the listed rule questions before implementing the affected mechanic.

## Mac preview builds and save identity

Build Mac previews with `bash scripts/build-mac-preview.sh`. Keep bundle ID `com.puffpuffreef.prototype` and the enabled app sandbox. Local ad-hoc signing applies the sandbox without Apple credentials. Never distribute a Mac preview built with `CODE_SIGNING_ALLOWED=NO`: it reads a different Application Support/UserDefaults location and previously made the user's patch-15 game appear to reset to patch 2.

The canonical Mac save is inside `~/Library/Containers/com.puffpuffreef.prototype/Data/Library/Application Support/puffpuff/`. The app imports its original sandboxed `PuffPuffReef.native.v1` defaults automatically. Do not copy saves between the Mac, an unsigned build, and the iPhone simulator or choose a save by the highest level number. Preserve originals during any recovery.

Before replacing the delivered Mac app, quit it and preserve the previous app under the workspace's `work/` directory. Copy the signed build without resource forks or extended attributes (`ditto --norsrc --noextattr`), verify its signature and sandbox entitlement, then reopen the delivered app and confirm it resumes the same board and resources. A freshly built preview should never silently start a new save for an existing player.

## Home, pause, and temporary level picker

Trail is Home. Launch into the saved close-up cove with a clear Bloom button in its heading. Bloom resumes the exact board; Pause → Home returns to Trail. Keep the quest world visible instead of a separate game-choice menu. No bottom navigation toolbar. Pause blocks input and freezes decorative clocks; a Trail swim settles at its saved destination. Keep Glow/shot balances in Pause, lives only in Bloom and Settings, and save-attention notices intact.

Settings → Debug levels provides Start from beginning and Start from level (typed 1–60), compiled only with DEBUG. The normal Trail → Bloom button resumes directly. Same-level choices resume the exact board. Jumps retain the original checkpoint; replacing a disposable replay is abandonment. Preserve original board, resources and Trail data. Normal progression stops after 60; levels 61–100 remain drafts. Never erase legacy progress above 60.

## Bloom completion

Use the golden coral animation and happy Puff Puff to communicate the win. Do not write Bloomed or add a separate completion card. Show the total Glow earned (3 normally, 4 including a pearl) and any earned extras inside translucent bubbles. They rise over the board during the fish swim, then dock beside the taps-left counter; do not preview the pearl target in a gameplay footer. Rendering never awards resources. Keep Next patch and Home. Resource balances still update normally. A fresh win plays one finite victory swim over the board using the official fish: four varied gentle swims (wiggle, swoop, bob and drift) for easier classic/pearl patches, a lap for stone/current/5×5 patches, and a bubble spin for star/mixed or high pearl-target patches. The shared coral completion clock drives the swim; it never grants rewards. Use one continuous fish layer across the header and board. Geometry anchors locate the real header resting frame and coral board in the same scrolling content. A 0.25-second departure leads into the swim, followed by a 0.5-second curved return with zero endpoint velocity. The existing swims are 50% shorter: wiggle/swoop/lap/spin last 2.05/2.25/2.65/3.25 seconds; bob/drift last 2.1/2.15 seconds. Match the exact header body anchor, size, rotation and happy pose; never fade out, swap fish views or teleport back. The shared ocean phase continues fins, eyes and breathing throughout and after arrival. Only bubble effects fade. The finite celebration clock lives in BloomView and also drives the coral ripple. Pause freezes it, Reduce Motion skips travel, and reopening a completed board does not replay it. After a fully watched swim, Next starts a 1.1-second geometric inflation in the header; skipping ahead goes directly to the puffed playing pose. Use the finite playback time to detect completion, so time spent paused does not count. Settings has a persistent Celebration animations switch (`puffpuff.celebrationAnimations`), default on. Turning it off cancels the fish swim/reinflation without suppressing the coral completion effect or earned bonus labels; re-enabling it never replays an old win. Tapping the header fish during play blows a short burst of little bubbles without changing taps, resources or the board. Its finite clock pauses with the game; Reduce Motion suppresses the burst.

## Bloom lives, lighters and Trail progress

Lives are loss-only (October 27 update): starting and winning cost nothing. Lose one when an unfinished attempt runs out of taps, is restarted or is discarded. Persist `lifeLossHandled` so continuing with more moves and failing/restarting again never double-charges that attempt. Returning to Trail, pausing and reopening keep the board and cost nothing. Debug jumps that preserve a checkpoint do not abandon it; discarding a replay does. Start with 5 lives, refill one every 5 minutes up to 5; gifts may exceed 5. A new attempt needs a positive balance or unlimited boost, but an already started board remains playable at zero. Restart after losing the last life creates a waiting board. Wins can continue indefinitely without losing lives.

Save format 12 preserves format-11 balances and marks already prepaid/grandfathered attempts handled, avoiding a second debit. New attempts have no upfront debit. Preserve atomic writes/rollback. Restart is labelled only Restart; tapping opens Are you sure? with the live count and a -1 icon when applicable. At zero, the restart-sheet ad grants 1 life and restarts in one saved transaction. The separate Lives & boosts sheet grants 3 lives without restarting. Both are explicitly free offline previews until a real rewarded-ad integration is requested.

Normal unlimited-lives gifts activate immediately and refill lives to at least five, preserving overflow. A current or new attempt protected by a boost remains protected through its end. Debug refills remain Settings-only. Do not restore redundant explanatory subtitles or routine reward footers.

A lighter consumes one item to turn one sleepy coral on and hold it on for the attempt, with no tap cost or neighbor changes. Its own tile can still be tapped to affect neighbors. Filter held-on targets from pulses and the hint solver so the puzzle remains solvable. Pebbles/gaps/lit tiles cannot consume it. Cancel is free. Restart removes the protection without refunding the item; save/checkpoints retain it. Completion from a lighter uses the normal reward transaction once.

Trail food/chest counts are small tappable progress bars. A finite pickup flight and count pulse focus attention on new food, with a sparkle for treasure. Use the journey clock, honor Reduce Motion/Pause, and never award anything from animation.
